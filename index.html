Ziwei (Sara) Gong

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at Columbia University under the supervision of Professor Julia Hirschberg, with expected graduation in July 2027.

My research focuses on multimodal learning at the intersection of NLP, speech processing, and psychology. My thesis is on modeling human affect and psychological dynamics in interaction.
